About Swiflora Logistics

Rooted in reliability, built for speed — modern global logistics, door-to-door parcel delivery, and smart supply chain management.

OUR STORY

Growing Global Freight with Speed & Transparency

Swiflora Logistics was built on a simple idea: global shipping should feel as dependable as handing a package to a neighbor. Today our network reaches partner carriers, warehouses, and last-mile fleets across dozens of countries, connecting air, sea, and road freight into one seamless chain.

We support businesses of every size — from independent online sellers shipping their first order to established enterprises coordinating container loads — with live tracking, automated customs paperwork, and security you can count on at every handoff.

Our Mission

Move every package safely and on time, with full live visibility and a lighter footprint on the planet.

Our Vision

To be the logistics partner businesses trust first for fast, transparent, technology-driven cross-border trade.
